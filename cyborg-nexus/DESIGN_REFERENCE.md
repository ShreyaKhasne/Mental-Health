# CYBORG // NEXUS — Design Reference

Rebuild the landing page to match the user's supplied reference image: cinematic full-width 3D cyborg hero; dark black/graphite UI; thin white/cyan borders; cyan primary glow; blue secondary glow; subtle violet highlight; compact Orbitron-style headings; modular sections arranged as premium product panels.

Required sections in visual order:
1. Hero — CYBORG // NEXUS, HUMAN × MACHINE, large realistic 3D cyborg.
2. THE SYSTEM — four technology cards: Neural Interface, AI Vision, Cybernetic Augmentation, Bio Sensors.
3. MAP THE MACHINE — full-body interactive 3D cyborg with hotspots.
4. AUGMENTATION — large cybernetic arm/product render and specification panel.
5. AI CORE — large glowing mechanical core with status metrics.
6. HUMAN × MACHINE — biological-to-cybernetic transformation visual.
7. FUTURE TIMELINE — 2026, 2030, 2035, 2040 milestones.
8. ENTER THE FUTURE — immersive futuristic environment and Initialize Simulation button.
9. ARE YOU READY TO EVOLVE? — final CTA and contact area.

Palette:
- Deep Black #050608
- Graphite #11151C
- Electric Cyan #00E5FF
- Tech Blue #2563EB
- Neon Violet #8B5CF6
- Light Gray #EEF2F7

Do not make it look like an AI dashboard. Prioritize cinematic 3D product presentation, clean spacing, premium typography, subtle HUD details, glass panels used sparingly, and polished micro-interactions.
